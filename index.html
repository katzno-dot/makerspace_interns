Internship Mission

The Makerspace

You are not expected to know how to use every tool. What matters is being curious, working carefully, acting responsibly, and helping make the makerspace clearer and easier for others to use.

Your Mission Trail

Eight stops between "new intern" and "trusted teammate." Tap any stop to jump there.

Mission 1

Join the Team

Discover what it means to be a makerspace intern. You don’t need to know everything on day one. What matters is staying curious, being observant, asking thoughtful questions, and building the research skills to find accurate information and keep learning. Just as important, you’ll learn to make safety part of everything you do, paying attention to your surroundings, following procedures, and knowing when to stop and ask for guidance.

Mission goal Become someone the team can trust.
  • RTake on real responsibilities
  • QAsk useful questions
  • LLook carefully before acting
  • SFollow safety boundaries
  • LLearn from mistakes & new evidence
  • CContribute something useful
Mission 2

Map the Makerspace

Explore every zone before you touch a single tool. A good intern reads the room first.

In every zone, notice:

  • What happens here
  • Which tools & materials belong
  • Who uses the space
  • What hazards may be present
  • What safety rules protect people & equipment
Mission goal Learn to read the space before using anything in it.
  • Design Tank
  • Materials
  • Digital Fabrication
  • Food Lab
  • Grow Lab
Mission 3

Plan the Tool Catalogue

Your class has an important assignment: build a Makerspace Tool Catalogue for users of the makerspace. It will help beginners understand the available tools and prepare to use them safely and effectively.

Mission goal Create a useful contribution that makes the makerspace easier to understand.

The catalogue helps future interns discover…

  1. What tools are available
  2. Where each tool is located
  3. What each tool is designed to do
  4. How it works
  5. What can be made or explored with it
  6. What safety preparation is required
Mission 4

Build the Tool Discovery Guide

Before your class begins cataloguing the tools, use these eleven questions as examples to help guide your investigation. They are suggestions, not a required checklist, and are meant to spark ideas for other useful questions you might ask about each tool. Flip each card to see why the question could be helpful.

Mission goal Develop a careful investigation process you can repeat all course long.
Mission 5

Complete the First Tool Investigation

Your team uses the Tool Discovery Guide for the first time, with close teacher support. Step through the process below.

Your first idea may not be correct — and that's part of the mission. Strong interns change their explanations when they discover better evidence.

Mission goal Turn an unfamiliar tool into a clear, trustworthy catalogue entry.
Mission 6

Review and Upgrade

After your first investigation, your team reviews the information you found and the Tool Discovery Process you used. Before adding anything to the catalogue, check that the information clearly explains what the tool is, what it does, and how to use it safely. Make any improvements needed before finalizing the catalogue entry.

Mission goal Make the investigation process clearer, safer, and more useful.
Mission 7

Lead a New Investigation

Choose a new unfamiliar tool and repeat the mission — this time with greater responsibility. You'll still need teacher approval for hands-on exploration, but your team owns more of the process.

Mission goal Show that your discovery process works with a new tool in a new situation.
  • Organizing the investigation
  • Dividing team roles
  • Finding reliable information
  • Planning safe tests
  • Deciding when to stop or dig further
  • Explaining your conclusions
Final Mission

Pass It On

Every completed investigation adds another entry to the Makerspace Tool Catalogue. Along the way, you’ll build confidence in approaching unfamiliar tools, investigating them responsibly, and explaining what you discover to others. The catalogue and Tool Discovery Process will become resources that help makerspace users understand what each tool is, what it does, and how to use it safely.

Final mission goalLeave behind clearer, safer guidance for everyone who uses the makerspace.
